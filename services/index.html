Services

Demolition first — clearing and site work when the lot needs more.

Breaking Ground is a Kathleen, Florida father-and-son crew for mobile home demolition, shed and barn removal, and the site work that follows. Use this hub to pick the page that matches your lot — then send photos for a written estimate.

Most calls start with a structure that has to come down. After the demo we can clear the lot, excavate stumps (not cosmetic grinding), or haul storm debris. We do not market unrestricted building demolition or licensed general-contractor scopes.

Core service area is Kathleen, Lakeland, and Polk County. Larger demolition jobs are considered farther across Central Florida when the photos and access make sense.
